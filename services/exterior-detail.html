Inside the service

What this detail entails.

Here is the typical work involved. The final checklist is tailored to your vehicle's condition and the agreed package, rather than promising one-size-fits-all results.

01 / SERVICE DETAIL

Body wash

Remove routine road film, dust, and surface grime using a careful exterior wash process.

02 / SERVICE DETAIL

Wheels & wheel faces

Clean visible brake dust and buildup from wheels and surrounding areas.

03 / SERVICE DETAIL

Tire finishing

Finish the tires to complement the look of the freshly cleaned body.

04 / SERVICE DETAIL

Exterior glass

Clean windows and mirrors on the outside.

05 / SERVICE DETAIL

Detailing touches

Address accessible edges and visible trouble spots as the car is finished.

06 / SERVICE DETAIL

Surface assessment

Identify areas that may need dedicated correction or protection beyond standard detailing.

* Exact products, techniques and add-ons are confirmed before service.

From arrival to finish

How the process works.

We review the vehicle first, agree on the scope, then work through the appropriate steps.

Inspect the finish and visible contamination

Wash exterior surfaces and wheels

Dry and complete wheel and tire finishing

Review glass and make a final exterior pass

What should you expect?

A visibly cleaner exterior. A normal exterior detail is not the same as machine paint correction, coating installation or scratch repair.

The details matter

Common questions.

Is this the same as paint correction?

No. Exterior Detail focuses on cleaning and appearance. Paint Correction uses polishing to address certain defects in the clear coat.

Will you remove all scratches?

Scratches and deeper paint defects usually require a separate assessment and are not guaranteed to disappear with washing.

Can I add coating later?

Yes, ask about the Ceramic Coating page for available protection packages and surface preparation.

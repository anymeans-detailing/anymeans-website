Inside the service

What this detail entails.

Here is the typical work involved. The final checklist is tailored to your vehicle's condition and the agreed package, rather than promising one-size-fits-all results.

01 / SERVICE DETAIL

Exterior wash & surface care

A careful wash to lift everyday dirt from the body and exterior surfaces.

02 / SERVICE DETAIL

Wheels & tires

Attention to visible wheel grime, tires, and the finishing touches around them.

03 / SERVICE DETAIL

Cabin vacuum

Seats, carpets, mats and accessible crevices receive interior vacuum attention.

04 / SERVICE DETAIL

Interior touch points

Dashboard, center console, doors and commonly touched surfaces are cleaned.

05 / SERVICE DETAIL

Interior & exterior glass

Windows and mirrors are cleaned for a clearer, finished look.

06 / SERVICE DETAIL

Final walkaround

The vehicle is checked for spots that need a second pass before handoff.

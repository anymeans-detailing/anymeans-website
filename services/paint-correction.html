The details matter

Common questions.

What's the difference between one-step and two-step?

One-step is a single polishing stage for lighter improvement. Two-step generally uses separate correction and refinement stages for a more intensive result.

Can you remove deep scratches?

Deep scratches that penetrate through the clear coat may not be safely removable by polishing. Inspection comes first.

Should I get a ceramic coating afterward?

Protection can be a sensible follow-up to polished paint. See the Ceramic Coating options and ask AMD what preparation is included.

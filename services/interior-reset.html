Inside the service

What this detail entails.

Here is the typical work involved. The final checklist is tailored to your vehicle's condition and the agreed package, rather than promising one-size-fits-all results.

01 / SERVICE DETAIL

Seats & accessible surfaces

Care is matched to the vehicle's cloth, leather or other interior materials.

02 / SERVICE DETAIL

Carpets, mats & footwells

Loose debris and dirt are removed from reachable carpeted areas.

03 / SERVICE DETAIL

Crevices & console

Extra focus on cup holders, console areas, door pockets and other high-use spots.

04 / SERVICE DETAIL

Dash, trim & door panels

Common touch points and interior trim are carefully cleaned.

05 / SERVICE DETAIL

Inside glass & mirrors

Interior windows and mirrors are wiped for a clear finish.

06 / SERVICE DETAIL

Stains & problem areas

Visible staining and heavier soil are assessed to determine whether deeper treatment is needed.

* Exact products, techniques and add-ons are confirmed before service.

From arrival to finish

How the process works.

We review the vehicle first, agree on the scope, then work through the appropriate steps.

Check upholstery and the condition of the cabin

Remove loose debris and vacuum accessible areas

Clean surfaces and address agreed trouble spots

Finish the glass and review the interior

What should you expect?

A cleaner, more comfortable cabin. Deep extraction, odor remediation and permanent stain removal should be discussed separately rather than assumed.

The details matter

Common questions.

Does ceramic coating prevent scratches?

No. Ceramic coating is not a substitute for careful washing or paint protection film, and it does not make the vehicle scratch-proof.

Is paint correction included?

Paint preparation needs vary by vehicle. Ask AMD what correction, if any, is included with the quote before proceeding.

Do I still need to wash the car?

Yes. Coated vehicles still require appropriate regular washing and maintenance.
